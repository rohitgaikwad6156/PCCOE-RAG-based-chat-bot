## 1. Autonomous Academic Calendar & Examination Rules (2026-2027)
1.1 Examination Structure:
- PCCOE operates under the Autonomous Academic Evaluation Framework. Each course is evaluated through:
  1. Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE / In-Sem): 40 Marks (comprising Quizzes, Assignments, Mid-Sem Test, and Course Projects).
  2. End-Semester Examination (ESE): 60 Marks (Comprehensive Theory Paper conducted by the Autonomous Examination Cell).
- Winter End-Semester Examination (ESE) Schedule: December 08, 2026 to December 22, 2026.
- Practical and Oral Examinations: November 28, 2026 to December 05, 2026.
- In-Sem Examinations (ISE): October 14, 2026 to October 18, 2026.
- Minimum Attendance: Mandatory 75% attendance in theory lectures and 80% in practical sessions to be eligible to appear for ESE.
- Passing Standard: Minimum 40% in In-Sem + End-Sem combined, with at least 40% separately in End-Sem Examination.

1.2 Spring Semester Schedule:
- Commencement of classes: January 11, 2027.
- Mid-Sem Exams: March 10, 2027 to March 15, 2027.
- Spring End-Semester Exams: May 12, 2027 to May 26, 2027.

## 4. Scholarships, Financial Schemes & MahaDBT Portals
4.1 Government Schemes (via MahaDBT Portal):
- **EBC (Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojna)**:
  - 50% Tuition Fee waiver and 50% Exam Fee waiver for Open/General category students with family annual income up to Rs. 8,00,000.
- **OBC / EWS Freeship & Scholarship**: 50% Tuition Fee waiver.
- **SC / ST / VJNT / SBC Category**: 100% Tuition Fee waiver as per Social Welfare Department Maharashtra rules.
- **TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme)**: 100% Tuition Fee waiver for merit students allotted under TFWS seats through CAP rounds.

4.2 College Institutional Aid & Awards:
- Best Outgoing Student Award & Meritorious Student Cash Prizes awarded during the Annual Gathering 'Swarang'.
