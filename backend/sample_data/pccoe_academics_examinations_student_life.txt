## EXAMINATION STRUCTURE (AUTONOMOUS)

### Evaluation Pattern:
Each course is evaluated through two components:

1. **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) / In-Semester Exam (ISE)**:
   - Total Marks: 40
   - Components: Quizzes, Assignments, Mid-Semester Test, Course Projects, Lab Performance
   
2. **End-Semester Examination (ESE)**:
   - Total Marks: 60
   - Comprehensive Theory Paper conducted by the Autonomous Examination Cell

### Passing Standards:
- Minimum 40% in In-Sem + End-Sem COMBINED
- Minimum 40% SEPARATELY in End-Semester Examination (ESE)
- Both conditions must be met to pass the subject

---

## ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS

- **Theory Lectures**: Minimum **75% attendance** required to be eligible to appear for End-Semester Examination
- **Practical Sessions**: Minimum **80% attendance** required
- Students with shortage of attendance may be detained from appearing in exams
