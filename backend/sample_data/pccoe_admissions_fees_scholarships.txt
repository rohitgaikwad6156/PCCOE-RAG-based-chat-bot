## FIRST YEAR B.TECH (FY B.Tech) ADMISSION PROCESS

### Admission Authority:
- Conducted via Centralized Admission Process (CAP) governed by DTE Maharashtra / State CET Cell, Maharashtra

### Eligibility Criteria:
- Passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects
- Third subject: Chemistry / Biology / Biotechnology / Technical Vocational
- Minimum 45% marks in 10+2 for General/Open category
- Minimum 40% marks for Reserved Category / EWS / PWD candidates from Maharashtra

### Entrance Exams Accepted:
1. MHT-CET (Maharashtra Common Entrance Test)
2. JEE Main (Joint Entrance Examination Main)

## SCHOLARSHIP & FINANCIAL AID SCHEMES

### Government Scholarships (via MahaDBT Portal - mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in):

#### 1. EBC Scholarship (Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulk Shishyavrutti Yojna):
- Eligibility: Open/General category students with family annual income up to Rs. 8,00,000
- Benefit: 50% Tuition Fee waiver + 50% Exam Fee waiver
- Application: MahaDBT portal

#### 2. OBC Freeship & Scholarship:
- Eligibility: Other Backward Class students
- Benefit: 50% Tuition Fee waiver
- Application: MahaDBT portal

#### 3. EWS Scholarship:
- Eligibility: Economically Weaker Section (EWS) students
- Benefit: 50% Tuition Fee waiver
- Application: MahaDBT portal

#### 4. SC Category Scholarship:
- Eligibility: Scheduled Caste students
- Benefit: 100% Tuition Fee waiver as per Social Welfare Department Maharashtra rules
- Applicable Departments: Samaj Kalyan Vibhag (mahaeschol.maharashtra.gov.in)

#### 5. ST Category Scholarship:
- Eligibility: Scheduled Tribe students
- Benefit: 100% Tuition Fee waiver
- Application: Maharashtra Government tribal welfare schemes

#### 6. VJNT / SBC Category:
- Eligibility: Vimukta Jati / Nomadic Tribes / Special Backward Class
- Benefit: 100% Tuition Fee waiver as per Social Welfare Department rules

#### 7. TFWS (Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme):
- Eligibility: Merit students allotted under TFWS seats through CAP rounds
- Benefit: 100% Tuition Fee waiver
- Seats: Limited TFWS seats in each branch, allocated via DTE CAP rounds

## ADMISSION PROCESS TIMELINE (APPROXIMATE)

1. MHT-CET / JEE Main entrance exam (March-April)
2. Result declaration and merit list
3. State CET Cell Maharashtra CAP Round 1 counseling
4. Document verification and seat allotment
5. Fee payment and admission confirmation
6. CAP Round 2 (for unfilled seats)
7. Institute Level (IL) admissions for remaining seats
8. Classes commence (typically late July / early August)
