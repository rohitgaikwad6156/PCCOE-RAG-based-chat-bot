## OVERVIEW OF PCCOE COLLEGIATE CLUBS

Various Collegiate Clubs are available at Institute level as a platform for students to showcase technical, leadership and organizational skills.

### Main Objectives of Collegiate Clubs:
- To enhance the technical competency of students through project-based learning
- To improve the employability of students by developing necessary skills like leadership, communication, project management, and administration
- To develop a healthy learning environment wherein students can work as a part of a team following professional ethics and responsibilities

## INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY STUDENTS ASSOCIATION (ITSA)

### Overview:
ITSA (Information Technology Students Association) is the premier departmental student organization of the Department of Information Technology at PCCOE Pune. ITSA bridges the gap between academic curriculum and current IT industry demands, fostering technical excellence, leadership, and professional teamwork.

### Key Activities of ITSA:
- **Inspiria**: The flagship annual national-level technical symposium organized by ITSA, featuring algorithmic coding contests, web/app hackathons, technical paper presentations, and UI/UX design challenges
- **Hands-on Technical Bootcamps**: Periodic workshops on Full-Stack Web Development (MERN/Next.js), Cloud Computing (AWS/GCP), DevOps, Git/GitHub mastery, and Generative AI application development
- **CodeCombat & Weekly Coding Leagues**: Competitive programming sessions on LeetCode/HackerRank preparing students for technical interviews at Microsoft, Barclays, Adobe, Veritas
- **Alumni Mentorship & Tech Talks**: Interactive sessions with PCCOE IT alumni from Silicon Valley and Indian tech hubs
- **Social Impact Initiatives**: Digital literacy drives for rural schools and community development projects

### ITSA Leadership Structure:
- Head of Department (HOD) - IT: Chief Patron
- Faculty Coordinator: Senior Assistant Professor, Department of IT
- Student Positions: President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer, Technical Head, Event Management Head, Public Relations & Social Media Lead, Webmaster

## NOTES ON TEAM KRATOS RACING (TKR)

Team Kratos Racing Electric (also known as TKR) is one of PCCOE's most celebrated collegiate clubs. The team:
- Participates in Formula Student Germany (one of the world's most prestigious engineering competitions)
- Has 40 members from multiple disciplines (multidisciplinary collaboration)
- Designs, manufactures, and tests electric race cars
- Competes in Formula Bharat and other national Formula Student events
- Supported by PCCOE Mechanical and ENTC departments
