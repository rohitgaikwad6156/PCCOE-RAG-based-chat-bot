## UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS (B.Tech / B.E.)

### 1. Computer Engineering
- Degree: B.Tech (Computer Engineering)
- Duration: 4 years
- Department Website: computer.pccoepune.com
- NBA Accredited: Yes (till 31st December 2028)
- Description: One of the most sought-after departments at PCCOE. Focuses on software development, algorithms, data structures, operating systems, databases, computer networks, and emerging technologies including AI, cloud computing, and cybersecurity.
- Student Association: CESA (Computer Engineering Students Association)
- Key Labs: Programming Lab, Software Engineering Lab, AI/ML Lab, Computer Networks Lab, Database Lab
- M.Tech Program: M.Tech Computer Engineering available
- Ph.D.: Ph.D. Computer Engineering available
- DTE Program Code: 617524510

### 2. Information Technology (IT)
- Degree: B.Tech (Information Technology)
- Duration: 4 years
- Department Website: it.pccoepune.com
- NBA Accredited: Yes (till 31st December 2028)
- Description: Focuses on software engineering, web technologies, database management, networking, cybersecurity, data science, and enterprise applications.
- Student Association: ITSA (Information Technology Students Association)
- Flagship Event: INSPIRIA (annual national technical symposium)
- Key Labs: Web Technology Lab, Network Security Lab, IoT Lab, Cloud Computing Lab, Data Analytics Lab
- M.Tech Program: M.Tech (AI & DS) available
- DTE Program Code: 617524610

### 3. Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering (ENTC / E&TC)
- Degree: B.Tech (Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering)
- Duration: 4 years
- Department Website: entc.pccoepune.com
- NBA Accredited: Yes (till 31st December 2028)
- Description: Covers analog/digital electronics, communication systems, embedded systems, VLSI design, signal processing, microcontrollers, IoT, and wireless communication.
- Student Association: ETSA (Electronics & Telecommunication Students Association)
- Key Labs: Analog Electronics Lab, Digital Electronics Lab, Embedded Systems Lab, VLSI Lab, Microwave Lab, Communication Lab
- M.Tech Program: M.Tech (E&TC) - VLSI Engineering available
- Ph.D.: Ph.D. Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering available
- DTE Program Code: 617537210

### 4. Mechanical Engineering
- Degree: B.Tech (Mechanical Engineering)
- Duration: 4 years
- Department Website: mechanical.pccoepune.com
- NBA Accredited: Yes (till 31st December 2028)
- Description: Covers thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, machine design, manufacturing processes, robotics, automotive engineering, and CAD/CAM.
- Student Association: MESA (Mechanical Engineering Students Association)
- Key Labs: Fluid Mechanics Lab, Heat Transfer Lab, CAD/CAM Lab, Metrology Lab, Manufacturing Lab, Dynamics of Machinery Lab
- Motorsports Teams: Team Red Baron (SAE Baja ATV), Team Kratos Racing Electric (Formula Student), Team Solarium, Team Ambush, Team Automatons, Team Maverick India, Team Anantam
- M.Tech Programs: M.Tech (Mechanical) - Design Engineering; M.Tech (Mechanical) - Computational Mechanics
- Ph.D.: Ph.D. Mechanical Engineering available
- DTE Program Code: 617561210

### 5. Civil Engineering
- Degree: B.Tech (Civil Engineering)
- Duration: 4 years
- Department Website: civil.pccoepune.com
- NBA Accredited: Yes (till 31st December 2028)
- Description: Covers structural engineering, geotechnical engineering, transportation engineering, environmental engineering, surveying, GIS, and construction management.
- Student Association: CESA-Civil (Civil Engineering Students Association)
- Key Labs: Fluid Mechanics Lab, Soil Mechanics Lab, Concrete Technology Lab, Survey Lab, Transportation Lab, Environmental Engineering Lab
- M.Tech Program: M.Tech (Civil) - Construction & Management
- Ph.D.: Ph.D. Civil Engineering available
- DTE Program Code: 617519110

### 6. Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) - CSE (AI-ML)
- Degree: B.Tech - Computer Science & Engineering (AI-ML)
- Duration: 4 years
- Department Website: aiml.pccoepune.com
- Description: Focuses on machine learning algorithms, deep learning, neural networks, natural language processing, computer vision, data science, and AI applications in industry.
- Key Labs: AI/ML Lab, GPU Computing Lab, Data Science Lab, Computer Vision Lab, NLP Lab

### 7. Computer Engineering (Regional)
- Degree: B.Tech (Computer Engineering - Regional)

### 8. Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology)

### 9. Civil Engineering (Regional)
- Degree: B.Tech (Civil Engineering - Regional)
- Duration: 4 years
- Department Website: pccoepune.com/civilregional/

### 10. Multidisciplinary Studies
- Program: Multidisciplinary Studies
