## PLACEMENT PROCESS

1. Pre-Placement Training: Aptitude, Reasoning, Verbal, Technical preparation
2. Personality Development & Communication Skills workshops
3. Mock Interviews and Group Discussions
4. Company Pre-Placement Talks (PPT)
5. Written Aptitude Test
6. Technical Interview Rounds
7. HR Interview
8. Offer Letter
